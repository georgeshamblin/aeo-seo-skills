# Citations & Mentions

## When to Use
- An AEO audit shows the brand cited nowhere but its own site
- Planning off-site work after the site itself is fixed
- A competitor keeps getting recommended and you need to know why

## Why this is half the game

When an engine answers "best accountant in Yuba City," it triangulates sources it
trusts more than any brand's self-description. A brand with a perfect site and zero
third-party footprint loses to a mediocre site mentioned in five independent places.
Run `ai-crawler-access` and the site fixes first — then this is the highest-leverage
remaining work.

## The citation stack (work top to bottom)

1. **Platform profiles** — Google Business Profile (see local-seo), Bing Places,
   Apple Business Connect, Yelp. These feed engines directly.
2. **Industry directories** — the ones real buyers use: bar association, CPA society,
   trade associations, franchise directories, BBB. One accurate profile in the right
   professional directory outweighs twenty generic ones.
3. **Local sources** — chamber of commerce, local business journals, city business
   listings, sponsorships that produce a linked mention.
4. **"Best of" lists** — find every existing "best [service] in [city]" article
   (search it yourself and ask the AI engines what they cite). Each list is an
   outreach target: a polite note with genuine credentials sometimes gets an add.
5. **Earned media** — a quotable owner + local reporters. One news quote is a
   permanent high-trust citation.

## Rules

- **Exact NAP everywhere** (see local-seo) — a citation with a mismatched phone
  number subtracts instead of adds.
- **Quality over count.** Ten real, maintained profiles beat 200 auto-submitted
  directory spams; bulk citation services create cleanup work, not authority.
- **Never buy reviews, never post fake ones, never astroturf Reddit/forums.** Beyond
  ethics: FTC fines are real (fake reviews and undisclosed endorsements), platforms
  purge and penalize, and one caught fake burns trust with engines permanently.
- **Track what engines actually cite.** When Perplexity or ChatGPT answers your
  target question, note the cited domains — that list IS the outreach plan.
- Mentions don't need links to count for AEO. An unlinked name-in-the-right-list
  still teaches models the association; take it.

## Cadence

This is drip work: 2–3 quality citations a month beats 30 in launch week. Keep a
tracker (source, URL, NAP used, date, status) in the client repo so anyone can pick
it up.
